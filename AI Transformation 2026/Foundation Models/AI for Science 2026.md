# AI for Science 2026

> New benchmarks for evaluating LLMs' scientific discovery capabilities have emerged. AI is being integrated into research processes across **biology, chemistry, materials science, and physics**.

## Scientific Discovery Benchmark

A recent arXiv paper (2512.15567) proposes a systematic benchmark for evaluating LLM scientific discovery:

- **Scenario-grounded evaluation** — Real research projects defined by domain experts, decomposed into modular scenarios
- **Four domains** — Biology, Chemistry, Materials Science, Physics
- **Modular structure** — Separate evaluation of hypothesis generation, experiment design, data analysis, and conclusion drawing

## AlphaResearch — Superhuman Algorithm Discovery

DeepMind's AlphaResearch used LLMs to **discover novel algorithms surpassing best-of-human performance**. It achieved significant improvements over AlphaEvolve, demonstrating AI's potential to function not just as a research tool but as an **autonomous researcher**.

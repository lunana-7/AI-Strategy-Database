# WEF — AI Governance Alliance Playbook

> The World Economic Forum's **AI Governance Alliance**, in collaboration with Accenture, produced the most actionable global framework for operationalizing responsible AI — the **"Advancing Responsible AI Innovation: A Playbook"** — offering nine plays that turn principles into enterprise practice.

---

## Overview

Published in 2025, the WEF AI Governance Alliance Playbook addresses a persistent gap: organizations have AI ethics principles, but few know how to implement them. The Playbook emerged from the WEF's AI Governance Alliance — a multi-stakeholder group of 300+ organizations including governments, tech companies, civil society, and academia.

The WEF also released companion reports on **AI Sovereignty** (2026) and the **AGILE Index** (2025) for measuring national AI governance readiness.

---

## The Nine Plays

### Play 1: Establish AI Governance Structure
- Create a cross-functional AI governance committee
- Define clear roles: AI ethics officer, risk manager, compliance lead
- Ensure board-level visibility and accountability

### Play 2: Develop Risk Classification Framework
- Classify AI use cases by risk level (aligning with EU AI Act categories)
- High-risk applications require enhanced oversight
- Risk assessment should be continuous, not one-time

### Play 3: Implement Responsible Design Practices
- "Ethics by design" — integrate ethical considerations from the start
- Mandatory bias testing and fairness audits before deployment
- Document design decisions and trade-offs

### Play 4: Ensure Data Governance and Quality
- AI governance starts with data governance
- Establish data lineage, quality standards, and consent frameworks
- Address bias in training data before it propagates to models

### Play 5: Build Transparency and Explainability
- Deploy model cards and datasheets for all production AI systems
- Provide appropriate levels of explanation to different stakeholders
- Maintain audit trails for AI decision-making

### Play 6: Establish Human Oversight Mechanisms
- Define when human-in-the-loop is mandatory vs. optional
- Create escalation paths for AI edge cases
- Ensure meaningful human review, not rubber-stamping

### Play 7: Monitor and Evaluate Continuously
- Post-deployment monitoring for drift, bias, and performance degradation
- Regular red-teaming and adversarial testing
- Feedback loops from users and affected communities

### Play 8: Manage Third-Party AI Risks
- Due diligence on AI vendors and partners
- Contractual requirements for responsible AI practices
- Supply chain transparency for AI components

### Play 9: Foster AI Literacy and Culture
- Organization-wide AI literacy training
- Create a culture where raising AI concerns is encouraged
- Regular communication about AI governance updates

## Companion Frameworks

### AGILE Index 2025
The AI Governance International Evaluation (AGILE) Index benchmarks national AI governance:

- Evaluates countries across 6 dimensions: regulation, ethics, inclusion, innovation, transparency, accountability
- 2025 rankings: Singapore, EU, UK, Canada, and South Korea lead in governance maturity
- The index reveals a growing gap between "AI governance leaders" and countries with minimal frameworks

### AI Sovereignty Report (2026)
"Rethinking AI Sovereignty: Pathways to Competitiveness through Strategic Investments":

- Nations must balance AI openness with strategic autonomy
- Sovereign AI investment extends beyond compute to data, talent, and governance
- **83% of enterprises** view sovereign AI as strategically important (aligned with Deloitte data)

### Global Regulatory Innovation Platform (GRIP)
Launched July 2025, GRIP brings together regulators globally to:

- Share best practices in AI governance
- Co-develop practical regulatory frameworks
- Enable agile governance that keeps pace with AI capabilities

## Why This Matters for Enterprise AI

The WEF Playbook is significant because it represents **global consensus** on AI governance practices — something that no single country or company can achieve alone. For enterprises:

1. **Regulatory preview** — The Playbook foreshadows likely regulatory requirements globally
2. **Practical implementation** — The nine plays are specific enough to actually implement
3. **Stakeholder credibility** — WEF backing lends legitimacy to governance investments
4. **Cross-border operations** — The framework addresses the challenge of operating AI across different regulatory environments

# Deloitte — State of AI in the Enterprise 2026

> Deloitte's 7th annual enterprise AI survey — covering **3,235 business and IT leaders** across 24 countries — captures the critical pivot from **ambition to activation**, with the most detailed data yet on agentic AI, physical AI, and sovereign AI adoption.

---

## Overview

Published in January 2026, Deloitte's "State of AI in the Enterprise" report is subtitled **"From Ambition to Activation"** — reflecting its central finding that enterprise AI has moved past the experimentation phase but is struggling to translate pilots into production-scale transformation. The report surveyed leaders across six industries in 24 countries between August-September 2025.

---

## Key Findings

### Workforce Access Explosion
- AI tool access expanded **50% in one year** — from <40% to ~60% of workers equipped with sanctioned AI tools
- This represents the fastest enterprise technology rollout in recent history
- However, access ≠ effective use: training and change management lag behind deployment

### The Production Gap
- Only **25% of organizations** have moved 40%+ of AI experiments into production
- **54%** expect to reach that level within 3-6 months — suggesting a major production scaling wave
- The gap between experimentation and production remains the central challenge

### Three Tiers of AI Maturity

| Tier | % of Companies | Description |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| **Deep Transformation** | 34% | Using AI to fundamentally transform business models |
| **Process Redesign** | 30% | Redesigning key processes around AI, keeping business models intact |
| **Surface Adoption** | 37% | Using AI with little or no change to existing processes |

### Agentic AI is the Next Frontier
- **85% of companies** expect to customize AI agents for their business needs
- Only **21%** report having a mature agent governance model
- The governance gap is the single biggest risk in the agentic AI transition
- Agent orchestration platforms are emerging as a new infrastructure category

### Physical AI and Sovereign AI
- **58%** already use physical AI (robotics, embodied AI, digital twins)
- **83%** view sovereign AI as strategically important
- Physical AI adoption projected to reach **80% within two years**
- Sovereign AI investment driven by data residency requirements and geopolitical concerns

### Productivity and ROI
- **66%** report productivity and efficiency gains from enterprise AI
- **84%** are increasing AI investments
- **78%** of leaders report greater confidence in the technology
- But: translating productivity gains into measurable financial returns remains elusive for most

---

## The "Untapped Edge" Framework

Deloitte introduces the concept of the **"Untapped Edge"** — the gap between current AI capabilities and the value organizations are actually capturing. The report identifies four barriers:

1. **Data readiness** — Most enterprises still lack unified, AI-ready data architectures
2. **Talent & skills** — The supply of AI-skilled workers cannot meet demand
3. **Governance maturity** — Especially for agentic and autonomous AI systems
4. **Change management** — Organizational resistance outpaces technical deployment

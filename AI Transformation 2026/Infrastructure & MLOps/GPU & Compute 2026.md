# GPU & Compute 2026

> **Liquid cooling** deployment to support NVIDIA's Blackwell architecture is expanding industry-wide. High-end GPUs remain the largest contributor to component market revenue growth in 2026.

## Hardware Evolution

### NVIDIA Blackwell
NVIDIA's latest Blackwell architecture sets a new standard for AI training and inference performance. Google Cloud announced Blackwell-based infrastructure expansion at NVIDIA GTC 2026.

### Liquid Cooling
Power and thermal demands of high-density GPUs are accelerating the **industry-wide shift to liquid cooling**. IREN's deployment of liquid-cooled systems represents this broader trend.

### Custom Accelerators
Hyperscalers (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Microsoft Maia, etc.) are **co-deploying custom accelerators** to optimize cost, power efficiency, and workload-specific performance.

## The Age of Inference Economics

A key 2026 shift: **the economic center of gravity moves from training to inference**. As models are deployed to production, inference costs become the dominant component of total AI infrastructure spend. Deloitte defines this as "The Age of Inference Economics."

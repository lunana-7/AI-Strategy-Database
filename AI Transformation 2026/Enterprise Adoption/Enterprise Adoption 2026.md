# Enterprise Adoption 2026

> Enterprise AI adoption is growing at the fastest rate in software history. The GenAI market surged from **$1.7B** in 2023 to **$37B** in 2025, capturing **6%** of the global SaaS market.

## Current State

**88%** of organizations use AI in at least one business function, and **71%** regularly use GenAI. However, **over 80%** of organizations report no measurable EBIT impact. This gap between adoption and value realization is the defining challenge of 2026.

More than **half of enterprise AI spending** in 2025 went to AI applications, indicating that modern enterprises prioritize immediate productivity gains over long-term infrastructure bets.

# EU AI Act 2026

> The EU's risk-based AI regulatory framework enters **full enforcement in 2026**. It serves as the global benchmark for AI regulation, fundamentally impacting how global enterprises deploy AI.

## Risk Classification System

The EU AI Act classifies AI systems into **four risk tiers**:

| Tier | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Unacceptable | Prohibited | Social scoring, real-time remote biometric identification |
| High Risk | Strict regulation | Hiring AI, credit scoring, medical diagnostics |
| Limited Risk | Transparency obligations | Chatbots (must disclose AI usage) |
| Minimal Risk | Self-regulation | Spam filters, AI gaming |

## Corporate Compliance Requirements

1. **AI Inventory Documentation** — Full catalog of all AI systems and risk classifications
2. **Risk Classification** — Determination of risk tier for each system
3. **Third-Party Audits** — External audits for high-risk systems
4. **Model Lifecycle Management** — Training data, model versioning, performance monitoring
5. **Transparency Reporting** — Explainability guarantees for automated decision-making

## New Challenges for [[Agentic AI 2026|Agentic AI]]

Autonomous agent systems pose novel challenges that existing frameworks struggle to classify. Defining accountability within an agent's decision-making chain is the central debate of 2026.

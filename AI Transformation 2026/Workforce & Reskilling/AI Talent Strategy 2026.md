# AI Talent Strategy 2026

> Enterprises are adopting **education as their #1 talent strategy**. Training — not role or workflow redesign — is the primary response to AI-driven talent strategy adjustment.

## Emerging Roles

AI Transformation is creating new specialized roles:

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **AI Engineer** | LLM fine-tuning, RAG systems, agent pipeline development |
| **Prompt Engineer** | Effective prompt design and optimization |
| **AI Ethics Officer** | Organizational implementation of [[Responsible AI 2026\|Responsible AI]] principles |
| **AI Product Manager** | AI product planning, value definition, roadmap management |
| **MLOps Engineer** | [[MLOps 2026\|ML pipeline]] deployment, monitoring, operations |

## Strategic Approaches

### Enterprise-Wide AI Literacy
Provide foundational AI literacy to all employees while delivering role-specific deep-dive training. The goal is developing talent that **collaborates with AI**, not just talent that can "use AI tools."

### Build vs. Buy vs. Borrow
- **Build**: Reskill existing talent to strengthen AI capabilities
- **Buy**: Recruit external AI specialists
- **Borrow**: Engage consultants/contractors for short-term expertise

Most enterprises blend all three, with **Build (internal training)** receiving the largest investment.

# McKinsey — The State of AI in 2025

> McKinsey's annual Global Survey on AI (2025) represents one of the most comprehensive assessments of enterprise AI adoption, covering **1,800+ respondents** across industries and geographies.

---

## Overview

McKinsey's "The State of AI in 2025" report reveals that AI adoption has reached a **tipping point** — 78% of organizations now use AI in at least one business function, up from 72% in 2024 and 55% in 2023. More significantly, **65% of organizations regularly use generative AI**, nearly double the percentage from ten months prior.

However, the report's most provocative finding is the growing gap between AI **deployment** and AI **value capture**. While adoption soars, only **26% of companies** report meaningful bottom-line impact from their AI investments.

---

## Key Findings

### Adoption Acceleration
- **78% of organizations** use AI in at least one business function (2025)
- **65% regularly use GenAI** — nearly 2x from early 2024
- Average number of business functions using AI increased from 1.9 to 3.1
- Top adopting industries: Technology, Financial Services, Healthcare

### The Value Gap
- Only **26% report meaningful EBIT impact** from AI initiatives
- **47% achieved cost reductions** but struggled to translate them to revenue growth
- High performers ("AI leaders") capture **3-5x more value** than peers
- The gap between leaders and laggards is **widening, not closing**

### What Differentiates AI Leaders
McKinsey identifies five characteristics of organizations that successfully scale AI:

1. **Executive sponsorship** — CEO or C-suite directly championing AI strategy
2. **Cross-functional teams** — Breaking silos between data science and business units
3. **Modern data infrastructure** — Unified data platforms enabling rapid experimentation
4. **Talent investment** — 2x more spending on upskilling existing workforce
5. **Governance frameworks** — Clear policies on responsible AI use

### GenAI-Specific Insights
- **Marketing & Sales** and **Software Engineering** remain the top GenAI use cases
- **Customer service automation** saw the largest YoY growth (+34%)
- Organizations using GenAI for **code generation** report 20-40% productivity gains
- **RAG architectures** emerged as the dominant enterprise GenAI pattern

### Risk & Governance
- **44% of organizations** report at least one negative consequence from AI (up from 34%)
- Top risks: **inaccuracy** (56%), **IP concerns** (35%), **cybersecurity** (32%)
- Only **21%** have comprehensive AI governance policies in place
- Regulatory pressure is accelerating governance adoption, especially in EU markets

---

## The "Trough of Disillusionment" Warning

McKinsey explicitly warns that enterprise AI may be entering a **trough of disillusionment** — a phase where inflated expectations meet operational reality. The report recommends:

- Shifting from "AI for everything" to **targeted, high-ROI use cases**
- Investing in **change management** alongside technology deployment
- Building **measurement frameworks** before scaling AI initiatives
- Treating AI as a **business transformation**, not a technology project

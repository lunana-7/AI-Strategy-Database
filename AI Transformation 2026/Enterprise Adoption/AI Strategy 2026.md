# AI Strategy 2026

> Enterprise AI strategy in 2026 is shifting from experimentation to systematic scaling. The "AI Studio" centralized model is becoming mainstream, with executive-led top-down programs expanding.

## Strategy Paradigm Shift

Enterprise AI strategy is transitioning from **distributed experimentation (bottom-up)** to **centralized execution (top-down)**. The "AI Studio" model — where executives select focused AI investments on core workflows and execute through talent and resources — is emerging as the dominant approach.

## What AI ROI Leaders Do Differently

Top-performing companies set fundamentally different goals:
- **Revenue growth creation** (50%) — Growth-centric rather than cost-cutting
- **Business model reimagination** (43%) — Transforming existing BMs to AI-native
- **Enterprise AI governance** — Leveraging [[AI Governance 2026|systematic governance]] as competitive advantage

## Challenges

**42%** of AI projects are cancelled, and only **16%** succeed in enterprise-wide scaling. Key failure drivers include unclear value definition, [[Workforce Transformation 2026|skills gaps]], and governance gaps.

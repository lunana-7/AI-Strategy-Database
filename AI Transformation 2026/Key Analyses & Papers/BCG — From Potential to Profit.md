# BCG — From Potential to Profit: Closing the AI Impact Gap

> Only **5% of organizations** have reaped substantial financial gains from AI — but they show **4x higher total shareholder returns** than AI laggards. BCG's definitive report on the widening value gap.

🔗 [Read the report](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/closing-the-ai-impact-gap)

## Key Findings

### The Widening Value Gap

| Segment | Description | Share |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| **AI Leaders (5%)** | Substantial financial gains, 4x TSR | 5% |
| **AI Experimenters (~55%)** | Some pilots, no material value | 55% |
| **AI Laggards (~40%)** | Minimal or no AI adoption | 40% |

The gap is **widening, not closing**. While 88% of organizations use AI, **60% generate no material value** despite investments. McKinsey corroborates: only 39% see EBIT impact.

### What the 5% Do Differently

1. **CEO-led AI strategy** — Nearly 75% of CEOs are the main AI decision maker (2x vs. last year)
2. **Invest in people, not just tech** — 10% of value from algorithms, 20% from technology, **70% from people and processes**
3. **Agent-first approach** — Leaders allocate 15% of AI budgets to [[Agentic AI 2026|agents]], driving 17% of total AI value (projected 29% by 2028)
4. **Measure ruthlessly** — Concrete KPIs tied to business outcomes, not AI metrics
5. **Scale fast** — Move from pilots to production in weeks, not months

### The 10-20-70 Rule

BCG's most cited finding: AI value distribution is **10% algorithms, 20% technology, 70% people and processes**. This fundamentally reframes [[AI Strategy 2026|AI strategy]] — organizations over-investing in models while under-investing in change management and [[Reskilling & Upskilling 2026|reskilling]] will fail.

## Critical Analysis

**Strengths**: Largest enterprise AI survey (1,800+ executives). The 10-20-70 framework is immediately actionable. Data directly contradicts the "just deploy better models" narrative.

**Weaknesses**: Self-reported survey data. "5% leaders" definition may be generous. Consulting firms have incentive to emphasize people/process (their core offering) over technology.

**Key Insight for Strategy**: If 70% of AI value comes from people and processes, then [[Workforce Transformation 2026|workforce transformation]] isn't a supporting initiative — it IS the AI strategy.

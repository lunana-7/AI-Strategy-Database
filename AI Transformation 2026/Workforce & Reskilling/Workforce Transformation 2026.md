# Workforce Transformation 2026

> According to McKinsey, up to **30% of current work hours could be automated** by 2030, with GenAI accelerating the timeline. Europe alone may need up to **12 million occupational transitions** — **2x** the pre-pandemic pace.

## Current State

AI-driven workforce transformation is unfolding not as simple job replacement but as **task redesign**. 40% of respondents expect **20%+ of their workforce** to be reskilled within three years.

The AI skills gap is identified as the **#1 barrier** to [[Enterprise Adoption 2026|enterprise AI adoption]], with education — not role or workflow redesign — being adopted as the top talent strategy response.

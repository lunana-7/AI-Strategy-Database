# AI ROI Measurement

> AI ROI measurement is one of the hottest executive challenges in 2026. Investment is surging, but only a handful of companies can actually measure and prove business value.

## Current State

Only **25%** of enterprises are achieving expected ROI, and **95%** of GenAI pilots fail to make it to production. However, companies that deployed [[Agentic AI 2026|AI Agents]] to production saw **74%** achieve ROI within the first year.

Only **29%** of executives say they can measure AI ROI, suggesting that measurement frameworks themselves are either absent or immature.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Expected ROI Achievement | 25% |
| GenAI Pilot Failure Rate | 95% |
| Agent Deployment ROI | 74% |
| Executives Able to Measure ROI | 29% |

## Paths to ROI Realization

1. **Pilot-to-Production Acceleration**: Systematic pathways from small experiments to enterprise-wide scaling
2. **Measurement Framework Development**: KPI systems covering cost reduction, revenue growth, and productivity gains
3. **Agent-First Approach**: Moving beyond chatbots to [[Agentic AI 2026|autonomous agent]] deployment for higher ROI

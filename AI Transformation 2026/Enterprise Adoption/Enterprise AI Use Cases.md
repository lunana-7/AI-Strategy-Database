# Enterprise AI Use Cases

> Over half of enterprise AI spending in 2025-2026 went to AI applications. The trend toward immediate productivity gains over long-term infrastructure investment is unmistakable.

## Top Use Cases by Spending

| Domain | Spending | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| AI Coding Tools | $4B | Over 50% of departmental AI spend |
| IT Operations | $700M | Infrastructure monitoring, failure prediction |
| Marketing Platforms | $660M | Content generation, personalization |

## Key Trends

**Coding Dominates**: AI tools are being applied across the entire software development lifecycle, with teams reporting **15%+** velocity gains. GitHub Copilot, Cursor, and similar tools are fundamentally changing developer productivity.

**IT Operations Automation**: AIOps handles incident detection, root cause analysis, and automated remediation, driving operational efficiency.

**Marketing GenAI**: Content generation, A/B test automation, and hyper-personalized marketing are the core application areas.

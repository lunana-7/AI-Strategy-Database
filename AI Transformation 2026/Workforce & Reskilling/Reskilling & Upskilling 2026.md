# Reskilling & Upskilling 2026

> McKinsey redefines reskilling not as simple training but as **"change management."** It must be approached as organizational transformation, not just learning programs.

## Core Competency Framework

### Technical Skills
- **AI Tool Integration** — Effectively embedding AI into work workflows
- **Data Literacy** — Data interpretation, quality assessment, decision-making
- **Risk & Security** — Understanding and responding to AI system threats
- **Ethics Protocols** — Practical application of [[Responsible AI 2026|AI ethics]] principles

### Uniquely Human Skills
- **Empathy** — Human interaction capabilities that AI cannot replicate
- **Critical Thinking** — Verification and judgment of AI outputs
- **Resilience** — Adaptability in rapidly changing environments
- **Creativity** — Driving innovation and novel problem-solving

### Human-AI Interaction Skills
- **Problem Framing** — Crafting effective queries for AI systems
- **AI Output Oversight** — Judging accuracy and appropriateness of results
- **Result Interpretation** — Contextualizing AI analysis within business frameworks
- **Exception Management** — Handling edge cases that AI cannot process

## Reskilling as Change Management

McKinsey's key insight: reskilling should be an **executive-led organizational transformation, not an L&D department program**. Successful reskilling requires more investment in organizational culture, incentive structures, and career path redesign than in learning content itself.

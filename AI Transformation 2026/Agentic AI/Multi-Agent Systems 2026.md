# Multi-Agent Systems 2026

> Both Forrester and Gartner see 2026 as the **breakthrough year for multi-agent systems**. Specialized agents collaborating under central orchestration is becoming the new standard.

## Multi-Agent Architecture

MAS (Multi-Agent Systems) replace monolithic AI with **multiple specialized agents collaborating under an orchestrator**:

- **Specialist Agents**: Domain-specific capabilities (data analysis, document processing, API calls, etc.)
- **Orchestrator Agent**: Task decomposition, agent assignment, result synthesis
- **Memory & State**: Shared context and state management across agents
- **Guardrails**: [[Responsible AI 2026|Safety]] and [[AI Governance 2026|governance]] constraints

## The 2026 Inflection Point

2026 marks the transition of MAS from **pilots to production**. However, only **2%** have achieved full-scale deployment. Unified infrastructure, standardized inter-agent communication protocols, and observability remain critical challenges.

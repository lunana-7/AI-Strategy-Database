# Manufacturing AI 2026

> In 2026, [[Agentic AI 2026|Agentic AI]] in manufacturing extends beyond the factory floor to **control entire industrial processes**. Predictive maintenance, supply chain optimization, and AI-powered robotics are the core pillars.

## Key Application Areas

### Predictive Maintenance
AI analyzes equipment sensor data in real-time to **detect anomalies before failure**. This dramatically reduces unplanned downtime and optimizes maintenance costs.

### Supply Chain Optimization
Comprehensive analysis of demand patterns, inventory levels, and supplier lead times to optimize supply chains. [[AI Agent Use Cases|AI Agents]] are evolving to autonomously handle purchase orders, inventory adjustments, and logistics routing.

### AI-Powered Robotics
AI robots automate complex, repetitive tasks. Combined with computer vision, they handle quality inspection, assembly, and packaging, with **Edge AI** technology enabling real-time decision-making.

## The Rise of Edge AI

Manufacturing environments demand real-time decisions where cloud round-trip latency is unacceptable. **Edge AI** runs inference directly on the factory floor, with hybrid edge-cloud architectures being the key [[AI Infrastructure 2026|infrastructure]] pattern.

## Key Technologies

| Technology | Role |
|------------|------|
| Edge AI | Real-time on-site inference |
| Digital Twin | Virtual simulation for optimization |
| Computer Vision | Quality inspection, safety monitoring |
| Agentic AI | Autonomous process control |

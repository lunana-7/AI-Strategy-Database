# AI Supply Chain 2026

> AI infrastructure supply chains are becoming **increasingly constrained** heading into 2026. Memory vendors are prioritizing higher-margin **HBM production**, limiting capacity for conventional DRAM and NAND used in AI servers, causing prices to spike sharply.

## The HBM Bottleneck

HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) is a critical component for [[GPU & Compute 2026|high-performance GPUs]]. As memory vendors (SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron) focus on HBM production:

- **Conventional DRAM** — Supply reduction for AI server memory
- **NAND Flash** — Storage price increases in tandem
- **Overall Memory Market** — Price spikes due to supply imbalance

## Power & Cooling Constraints

Surging AI infrastructure power demands are making data center site selection and power procurement a top strategic issue:

- **Power Density** — Blackwell GPU single-rack power consumption dramatically higher than previous generations
- **Cooling Costs** — Upfront investment for liquid cooling transition
- **Site Competition** — Intensifying competition for data center sites with reliable power supply

## Geopolitical Risk

Geopolitical concentration of the semiconductor supply chain (TSMC foundry, HBM 3-company oligopoly) elevates supply risk. Nations are investing in domestic semiconductor production capacity, but near-term supply constraints are expected to persist.

# Foundation Models 2026

> Foundation model development in 2025-2026 is centered on **reasoning optimization**. DeepSeek-R1's RLRF, GPT-5-level expert performance, and Memory-R1's reinforcement learning with external memory are the major breakthroughs.

## Key Models & Innovations

| Model/Research | Core Innovation | Released |
|----------------|-----------------|----------|
| GPT-5 | Expert-level math/programming performance | OpenAI, 2025 |
| DeepSeek-R1 | RLRF (Reinforcement Learning with Reasoning Feedback) | DeepSeek, 2025 |
| Gemini 2.5 | Advanced multimodal reasoning | Google, 2025 |
| Memory-R1 | RL framework for external memory management | arXiv, 2025 |
| AlphaResearch | Superhuman algorithm discovery | DeepMind |

### The Reasoning Revolution
[[Reasoning Models 2026|Reasoning models]] have emerged as the new competitive axis for LLMs. Beyond Chain-of-Thought, reinforcement learning-based reasoning optimization is the focus.

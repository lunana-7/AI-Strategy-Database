# Agentic AI 2026

> Gartner predicts **40% of enterprise applications will embed AI Agents** by the end of 2026 (up from <5% in 2025). The agentic AI market is projected to grow from $7.8B to **$52B+** by 2030.

## Current State

Agentic AI is evolving beyond simple chatbots into **AI systems that autonomously set goals and execute tasks**. However, only **6%** of organizations have achieved full-scale deployment, with the majority still in pilot stages.

By 2028, agentic AI is expected to create **$450B in economic value**, with customer service, financial research, and IT automation leading the way.

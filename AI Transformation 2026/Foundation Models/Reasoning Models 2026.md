# Reasoning Models 2026

> DeepSeek-R1 achieved complex reasoning performance surpassing traditional RLHF through **RLRF (Reinforcement Learning with Reasoning Feedback)**, opening a new era of reasoning-focused models.

## RLRF — The Core Innovation

DeepSeek-R1's key contribution is **RLRF**, an evolution of RLHF:

| Aspect | RLHF | RLRF |
|--------|------|------|
| Feedback Source | Human preferences | Reasoning correctness |
| Optimization Target | Overall response quality | Logical reasoning process |
| Strength | Natural conversation | Math/science reasoning |
| Weakness | Complex reasoning limits | Potential subjective judgment weakness |

## Key Reasoning Models

1. **DeepSeek-R1** — RLRF-based, state-of-the-art in math/science domains
2. **GPT-5** — Expert-level math/programming performance
3. **Gemini 2.5** — Advanced multimodal reasoning
4. **Claude Opus 4** — Long-context reasoning and coding specialization

## Beyond Chain-of-Thought

From early Chain-of-Thought prompting, models have evolved toward **internalizing reasoning processes within the model itself**. This is critical for [[AI for Science 2026|scientific discovery]] and complex decision-making in [[Agentic AI 2026|autonomous agents]].

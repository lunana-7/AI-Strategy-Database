# Data Quality & Readiness 2026

> Data quality is the single most critical factor determining AI success in 2026. **72% of companies** will prioritize data foundations and pipelines for AI capabilities over the next 12 months, yet only **1 in 5** have actually achieved data readiness.

## The Data Quality Imperative

Poor data quality is the root cause of most AI failures:
- **Hallucinations** — LLMs generating false information from noisy training data
- **Biased predictions** — Models inheriting biases from incomplete or skewed datasets
- **Inconsistent recommendations** — Contradictory outputs from poorly integrated data sources

Organizations implementing **continuous governance monitoring** see **40% fewer model failures** compared to those relying on static policies.

## Data Readiness Dimensions

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Completeness** | No critical gaps in data fields or records |
| **Accuracy** | Data correctly represents real-world entities |
| **Consistency** | Uniform formats and definitions across systems |
| **Timeliness** | Data is current enough for its intended use |
| **Accessibility** | Authorized users can find and use data efficiently |
| **Lineage** | Full traceability from source to consumption |

## Investment Trends

**86%** of companies plan to increase data management investments this year, focusing on privacy, security, governance, and employee upskilling. The shift is from treating data as a byproduct of operations to treating it as a **strategic asset** that directly determines AI ROI.
